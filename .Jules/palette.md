## 2024-05-20 - Adding ARIA labels and focus states to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without clear text labels are difficult for screen reader users to understand, and lack of visual focus indicators makes keyboard navigation difficult.
**Action:** When adding or updating icon-only buttons, prioritize ensuring they have descriptive `aria-label`s and clear visual focus states (`focus-visible:ring-2`) to guarantee accessibility and usability for all users.
